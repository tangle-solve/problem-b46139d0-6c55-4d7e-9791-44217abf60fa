### CHEM-000523: Chemically reproducible thorium-229 nuclear clocks
- **Subfield:** Nuclear, actinide and superheavy-element chemistry
- **Open:** Control host composition, dopant sites and defects so independently prepared solid-state nuclear clocks agree with smaller, fully budgeted systematic uncertainties.
- **Status:** Ooi and colleagues measured 220 Hz reproducibility, approximately \(1.1\times10^{-13}\) fractionally, across two differently doped CaF₂ crystals in 2026. Their temperature-insensitive operating point addresses one variation source.
- **Type:** experimental | engineering
- **Why:** Reproducible host chemistry is necessary for deployable nuclear frequency standards.
- **Key works:** T. Ooi et al. (2026), *Frequency reproducibility of solid-state thorium-229 nuclear clocks*, [DOI:10.1038/s41586-025-09999-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09999-5).
